About Us

About Us

"Gupta Export is dedicated to transforming hospitality spaces into cultural destinations through the timeless art of Jodhpur woodworking."

A Legacy of Craft, A Future of Design

Founded in 2001, Gupta Export has evolved from a passionate atelier into a respected authority in artisanal furniture. Rooted in Jodhpur—India's eminent furniture heartland—our work bridges the gap between age-old craftsmanship and a refined, contemporary design language.

In-House Precision

Every piece is the result of rigorous material selection and skilled hands. By overseeing the entire lifecycle in-house—from raw sourcing to final finishing—we ensure every item meets the exacting standards of the global hospitality and retail sectors.

Ethical & Global Standards

We believe that how a product is made is as important as the product itself. As an AGCS Verified Facility, we are committed to the highest levels of social and ethical compliance, ensuring our manufacturing processes meet international quality benchmarks.

Partnering in Excellence

"Our creations grace premium hotels, boutique restaurants, and curated hospitality spaces—from high-energy breweries and bars to elite Airbnb retreats across the country. Beyond the furniture, we define ourselves through enduring partnerships built on trust, absolute transparency, and a shared pursuit of excellence."
